Theory of Constraints — solving one bottleneck reveals the next. Simulate resolution sequence and cascade effects.
How to read this chart: Each node is an overloaded resource — size reflects overload volume, color shows severity (red ≥120% · amber ≥100%). Arrows connect resources that share products in their routing: resolving the upstream bottleneck frees throughput that immediately loads the next resource downstream. ■ Green = resolved by selected shift option  ·  ■ Orange ! = newly revealed overload after upstream resolution  ·  ■ Grey = already within capacity.
To simulate: click any node to cycle through shift options (Sat → Night → Combined), or use the dropdowns on the right. Totals and the cascade update live. ⚡ Auto-Resolve greedily picks the cheapest option that resolves each overload, ordered by revenue-per-cost ratio.
